# CarSpotter — 14-Day Short-Form Content Pack

Organic TikTok / Reels / YouTube Shorts. NOT ads — these look native so they
actually get pushed by the algorithm.

## The only rules
1. **Post 1 per day.** Same time every day (evening is fine). Consistency > perfection.
2. **Hook in the first 1 second.** The on-screen text at 0s is the whole game. If they don't stop scrolling in 1s, nothing else matters.
3. **Film vertical, 9:16, in daylight or bright light.** 15-25 seconds each.
4. **Same caption formula every time:** `[hook] 👀 link in bio` + the hashtag block at the bottom of this file.
5. **Batch film.** Sit down once, shoot 5-7 clips in an hour, post them across the week. You are NOT filming daily — you're posting daily.

You film. I wrote every word that goes on screen + every caption. Just read them off.

## DAY 1 — "What's that car?" (the flagship)
- **Film:** Walk up to any nice/weird/expensive car on the street. Phone up, scan it in the app, show the result card.
- **0s on-screen text:** `POV: you finally know what that car is`
- **3s:** screen-record the scan → result card (make, value, rarity)
- **End text:** `2 seconds. that's it.`
- **Caption:** what's that car? now you'll always know 👀 link in bio

## DAY 2 — Price reveal (loop bait)
- **Film:** Point at a car. Text on screen asks the viewer to guess the price BEFORE the reveal.
- **0s:** `guess how much this costs… (most people are WAY off)`
- **Mid:** scan → reveal value range. React.
- **End:** `were you close? 😅`
- **Caption:** I was off by $200k 💀 link in bio

## DAY 3 — Rarity flex

- **0s:** `this car is rarer than you think`
- **Mid:** scan → "Rarity 9/10" → "only ___ ever made" trivia from the result.
- **Caption:** the rarity score is my favorite part. link in bio

## DAY 4 — Parking lot speedrun

- Weeks 1-2: most clips get 200-800 views. Normal. You're training the algorithm.
- One clip will randomly pop (5k-50k+). When it does: **make 3 more clips exactly like it.** That's the whole strategy — find what hits, repeat it.
