# CarSpotter — Logo prompt for ChatGPT (image generation)

Paste any of these into ChatGPT (GPT-4o image / DALL·E). Generate, then ask for
tweaks ("make the gradient more cyan", "thinner lines", "no text version").

## Primary prompt (app icon)
> A modern app icon for "CarSpotter", an AI car-identification app. A minimalist
> camera viewfinder/reticle with four corner brackets framing a sleek sports-car
> silhouette in the center, viewed from a 3/4 front angle. Bold cyan-to-violet
> gradient (#22D3EE to #A855F7) on a near-black background (#05050B), subtle neon
> glow. Flat, geometric, clean vector style, rounded-square icon, centered,
> generous padding, no text. High contrast, premium, futuristic synthwave feel.
> Crisp edges, suitable for an iOS App Store icon.

## Wordmark prompt (logo with text)
> A horizontal logo for "CarSpotter". Left: a small rounded-square mark with a
> cyan-to-violet gradient (#22D3EE → #A855F7) containing a white camera-reticle
> circle with a tiny car silhouette. Right: the word "CarSpotter" in a bold,
> rounded geometric sans-serif, white, tight letter-spacing. Dark background.
> Clean, modern, tech-startup branding, vector, balanced spacing. Provide on a
> transparent/dark background.

## Variations to try
- "...a single continuous neon line drawing a car + camera reticle in one stroke."
- "...badge/emblem style, circular, like a car-club crest but neon and modern."
- "...replace the car with just headlights + a viewfinder for a more abstract mark."

## Brand specs (keep consistent)
- Gradient: cyan `#22D3EE` → violet `#A855F7`
- Background: ink `#05050B`
- Motif: camera viewfinder/reticle + car silhouette
- Vibe: synthwave, neon, premium, minimalist
- Font feel: bold rounded geometric sans (e.g. similar to SF Rounded / Poppins)

## After you pick one
Export 1024×1024 PNG (no transparency) for the App Store icon. For in-app +
web, also export a transparent-background SVG/PNG of the wordmark.
